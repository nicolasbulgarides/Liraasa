# 2100 Dreams: vocabulary candidates

From the draft translation (ADR-0027, `translation/`). These are **candidates for the author**, not words of Liraasa: the English words the draft most often had to paraphrase, and the compounds it coined more than once. A new root, a new gloss or a coinage becomes Liraasa only through a patch the author approves (SPEC §14).
