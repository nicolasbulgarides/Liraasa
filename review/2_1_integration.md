# Liraasa 2.1 integration: findings for the author

Built 2026-10-18 from `data/proposals/liraasa_2_1_patch.json` (ADR-0017).
Nothing here is resolved by the tool: each item is yours to keep, change or
veto.

## What was built

- `data/lexicon/liraasa_2_1.json`, from frozen 2.0 by
  `data/changes/2026-10-18-lexicon-2-1.json` (`npm run integrate:2.1`): 1462
  headwords (472 roots, 990 compounds), 10,234 forms, all distinct and all
  regenerated by rule; 106 lexicalised derivations; 391 attested image
  compounds; rulings 2.1:G46–G59.
- Every pair the patch lists builds exactly as listed: 30 roots, 145
  compounds, 6 listed derivations, 96 attested image compounds, the 7 renamed
  pattern modifiers and 3 heads, and the 39 respelled example sentences. The
  changeset generator halts if one does not.
- No id changed. `taha` and `volda` became the compounds `kezupago` and
  `larbopagobudi` in place, keeping their ids, glosses and empty slots, so a
  learner's cards and your overrides follow them.
- Validation (`npm run validate:2.1`): **22 PASS, 3 WARN, 0 FAIL**. The
  warnings are listed below.

## 2. New readings

- **`kezupago`** (country) is also `kezu` + `-pago`, "the place of land". 2.1:G59
  makes the compound reading canon, so validate.py check 3 lets it stand.
- **`kezupagolelde`** (from `tahalelde`) now splits two ways:
  `kezu` + `pagolelde` · `kezupago` + `lelde` (check 17, WARN). The lexicon
  records the intended split, `kezupago` + `lelde`.

of 2.1:G46 was applied to the lexicon's own direct questions: the question
word moves to where its answer would stand, and a final `ki` is added. A
ki-word of time stays first, as a frame. Each rewrite is an op in the
changeset, and the earlier text is kept in `handoff/liraasa_2_1_complete.json`
as `liraasa_2_0`.

**Left for you:**

## 4. `sola` in two function-word examples

`miyo`: "Miyo baba sola." (There is solitude.) and `loya`: "Li sola loya
benasan." (The solitude becomes good.). In 2.0, `sola` was not a word at all.
2.1 makes it "sun" (respelled from `sulba`), so these now read "there is a
sun" and "the sun becomes good", against their English. **Question:** what
did you mean these to say? `solo` is stone, so it may be a different word.

## 5. The song

*Nemelira de devi* is a 2.0 text and is not edited. Per 2.1:G58 it reads under
2.0 spellings. The guide shows it as written and prints the 2.1 spelling
beneath each respelled word: `harmavurlo` → `harmadesi`, `sulbanevra` →
`solanevra`, `ramuvorba` → `ramuduha`. **Question:** keep it a 2.0 text, or
make a 2.1 version?

## 6. Everything else that moved with the respelling

- **Older rulings' prose.** The earlier rulings' text and examples that named
  a respelled word are respelled with it. Example: 1.0:G17 now reads
  `la vuzinasan` = unexpectedly. Historical records keep their spellings:
  `history_0_8`, `legacy`, the 0.8 snapshot, the 1.0 changelog, the settled
  0.8 questions.
- **The reserved root bank.** Seven bank roots now carry meanings and left the
  bank: `puku`, `goza`, `duha`, `rupu`, `kezu`, `vugo`, `diyi`.
- **The retired 2.0 spellings.** They are reserved through
  `legacy.respelled_roots` and `legacy.migration_forms`, which every checker
  reads. The sound ledger gets no new rows: the patch does not say when, if
  ever, they may be reused.
- **The corpus.** Learn reads the six songs and *Bumrepago* in 2.1 spelling,
  from `data/corpus/songs_2_1` and `bumrepago_2_1`, 35 lines respelled
  (`data/corpus/corpus_migration_2_1.json`). The 1.0 originals are untouched.
- **Rulings G46–G59.** Their text is the patch's, word for word. Where the
  text names an earlier ruling, the 2.1 ruling records it as refined:
  1.0:G22, 0.8:R6, 1.2:G27, 1.2:G32, 1.2:G31.
