Nemelira de devi
 The Goddess's Lament
Maivo de amor, taivo po nevrania da li navuasa ala li teru gase baba velusolo.
 My love, you descended from the firmament to the earth like a falling star-stone.
Taivo po yugeya baba daigosan yuge, ata li veluri po lasola.
 You took on a mortal body, and the stars fell silent.
Eli-krono taivo ke lemvosan en li cilniri de li nelari.
 Now you are weary in the cities of humankind.
Taivo de mavolumi ka velusan, nara taivo de yuge ke vulrasan.
 Your soul is starlit by nature, but your body is wounded for now.
Maivo vuda vimova taivo de timruri raho ralnora gase renli.
 In dreams I see your tears, which fall like rain.
Li harmavurlo tivrota taivo, ata li kronorivla ne teklata.
 Temptation holds you, and the flow of time does not stop.
Taivo lemula li lorenuravu de li nelari ata li vemineme.
 You learn the anguish of humankind and the sorrow of the world.
Taivo de manliri yanbaya, ata taivo de lore ke lemvosan.
 Your hands grow old, and your heart is weary.
Maivo lavbula vobi taivo hupi li sulbanevra, nara maivo de valla ke lasosan.
 I pray for you above the sunset, but my voice is silent.
Kasa maivo ka devi, maivo ne povi venreva ala taivo.
 Because I am a goddess, I cannot come to you.
Li daigo ka baba dorbi, ata maivo sulnosia taivo en li lumiasa.
 Death is a door, and I wait for you in the Divine Light.
Maivo de lunosavali loki ke su taivo de rezari.
 My moonlit kiss still rests upon your lips.
Li velumelo loki lirala taivo de nole ultra li navu.
 The music of the spheres still sings your name beyond the sky.
Maivo de ramuvorba ka gase dendusan gase li vamrisevu.
 My longing for what is gone is as deep as the dark between the stars.
Taivo kersoka en li narvo gase baba nolsakerso su li lamsa.
 You grow in the darkness like new shoots upon the ashes.
Kube taivo relnura, maivo futu lomola taivo, ata li lektaasa futu venreva.
 When you return, I will embrace you, and the great reunion of all souls will come.
