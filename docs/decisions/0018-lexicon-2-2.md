# ADR-0018 — Liraasa 2.2: the grammar of the album

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Phase:** 2.2

## Context

The author's 2.2 patch (`data/proposals/liraasa_2_2_patch.json`, with the
brief `LIRAASA_2_2_PATCH.md`) brings the grammar in line with the published
album:

- **Rulings 2.2:G60–G68:**
  - G60: either question order is valid;
  - G61: exclamatives, narrowed;
  - G62: `tonsi` + adjective;
  - G63: `fime` + `nosi`;
  - G64: headless `raho`;
  - G65: adjective + `sidu` + verb;
  - G66: `kroti` / `krotiapo` before a clause;
  - G67: side nouns as prepositions;
  - G68: the album as a reference corpus.
- **Superseded:** 2.1:G46, G47 and G54 in full, and one clause of 0.8:G4.
- **Restored:** the 2.0 spelling `silro`, undoing the 2.1 respelling `puzu`.
- **Filled:** two empty forms, `soloasa` and `tarnulinusan`.
- **Imported:** the album (13 texts) and its 16 album readings.

The preflight the brief asks for passed. 2.1 is applied, and its G46, G47 and
G54 are word for word the 2.1 patch's text, so they were superseded without
asking. The author confirmed both proposed glosses on 2026-10-19.

## Decision

### 1. 2.2 is built from frozen 2.1 by one changeset

`npm run integrate:2.2` reads `liraasa_2_1.json` and halts unless its sha256
is `3655d48b…be63e9`. It applies `data/changes/2026-10-19-lexicon-2-2.json`,
made from the patch by `scripts/make_changeset_2_2.ts`. The generator halts
unless:

- every revert pair the patch lists builds (7 root forms, 12 compounds,
  8 image compounds, and the pattern modifier);
- no `puzu` form survives;
- both filled glosses are the ones the author confirmed.

The changeset's summary is the patch's `edition_note`, verbatim.

### 2. Superseding keeps the old ruling

A new op, `supersede_ruling`, marks an earlier ruling `superseded_by`, or
with `superseded_in_part: [{clause, by}]` for 0.8:G4, whose quotation clause
stands. Nothing is deleted, renumbered or reworded. The new rulings carry
`supersedes`. The LLM reference gives a superseded ruling
`status: SUPERSEDED … do not follow`.

The 2.2 rulings' examples are stored as `[liraasa, ""]`: the patch gives no
English, and none is invented in the data.

### 3. A respelling undone (silro)

`respell_root` puzu → silro, under the id the root has always had. The
respell code now recognises an undo:

- legacy rows that would lead a spelling back to itself are dropped;
- the 2.1 row in `legacy.respelled_roots` stays as history, marked
  `reverted: true`, and reserves nothing;
- `puzu` and its forms are retired spellings, read through
  `legacy.migration_forms`, so 2.1 texts stay readable.

`check_sounds.py` skips reverted rows. validate.py runs unmodified on a copy
of the lexicon without them, and `executed_against` records that.

### 4. The album is a corpus, not lexicon data

`data/corpus/album_2_0/` holds three files:

- `liraasa_album_corpus.json`: the recorded text, never edited;
- `album_readings.json`: AR-01…AR-16, copied verbatim from the patch;
- `album_current.json`: generated by `npm run build:album`.

`album_current.json` gives each line's current spelling, recomputed from the
live lexicon (the respellings since 2.0, through the same `correctText` every
migration uses), and places each reading on its line. The recomputation
agrees with the patch's precomputed `spelling_2_2` on every line.

The guide adds a part, "The album", after the Nemelira chapter. The LLM
reference adds an `album` section: 2.0 text, each line's current spelling
where it differs, and every album reading flagged `do_not_imitate` with its
standard form. No English is invented anywhere.

### 5. Validation

`scripts/validate_2_2.py` gives **23 PASS, 2 WARN**: the 2.0 kept
ambiguities, and the `kezupagolelde` split. Check 25 accepts only the
recorded changes:

- the supersede marks;
- the two filled slots;
- the restored spelling.

`tests/golden/lexicon_2_2.test.ts` runs the brief's step 5:

- every word of the G60–G68 examples resolves;
- every non-null standard form resolves;
- every album line resolves under the 2.0 reading, and the two filled forms
  are now words;
- no two forms share a spelling;
- no `puzu` form survives outside the legacy record;
- the build is reproducible byte for byte.

### 6. The tools read 2.2

Every tool now reads 2.2:

- the **editor**, the **checker** and **Learn**: the 2.1 → 2.2 diff is 21
  renamed and 2 reglossed;
- the **MFA export**, the **handoff** and the **LLM reference**;
- the **guide**: `Liraasa_2_2_Reference_Grammar`.

The **checker** follows G60–G67:

- a fronted question word is valid;
- a yes/no question must end in `ki`;
- exclamations are narrowed;
- a question opened by `raho` is an error.

**Learn's corpus** is carried into 2.2 spelling by `scripts/migrate_corpus.ts`.

## Consequences

- **The numbers.** 1462 headwords (472 roots, 990 compounds); 4034
  meaningful forms (+2); rulings through 2.2:G68.
- **Open for the author** (`review/2_2_integration.md`):
  - the four album readings marked `author_confirm`;
  - English for the ruling examples and the album;
  - two held questions (`ke sidu VERB`, gapped relatives of time or place).
