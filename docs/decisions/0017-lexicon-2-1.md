# ADR-0017 — Liraasa 2.1: thirty roots respelled, and the grammar rulings G46–G59

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Phase:** 2.1

## Context

The author's patch, `data/proposals/liraasa_2_1_patch.json` ("Liraasa 2.1",
based on the 2.0 LLM reference), does three things:

- it respells 28 roots;
- it replaces two roots by compounds: `volda` → `larbopagobudi`, `taha` →
  `kezupago`;
- it adds rulings 2.1:G46–G59.

Rulings G46–G56 are grammar: questions keep their words in place, then
exclamatives, blessings, N times, different-subject complements, evidentials,
relatives on a prepositional object, partitive `de`, result clauses, duration
and prohibitions. G57 is guidance for new spellings, and G58–G59 rule the
respelling. The patch lists every form, compound, derivation, image compound
and example sentence the respelling moves, closes four open questions and
opens two. The author asked for it to be implemented in full.

## Decision

### 1. 2.1 is built from frozen 2.0 by one changeset

`npm run integrate:2.1` (`scripts/integrate_2_1.ts`):

- reads `data/lexicon/liraasa_2_0.json` and halts unless its sha256 is
  `f958e760…706ae2`;
- applies `data/changes/2026-10-18-lexicon-2-1.json`;
- audits the result;
- writes `liraasa_2_1.json` and `integration_report_2_1.json`.

The build is deterministic, and a test rebuilds it byte for byte. The 2.0
word-building rulings stay on, and 2.1 mints no id.

The changeset is made from the patch by `scripts/make_changeset_2_1.ts`.
Nothing the patch lists is copied: forms are generated by the verb rule and
carried by the respelling code. The generator applies its own changeset and
halts unless every old → new pair the patch lists comes out exactly so:

- 30 roots, 145 compounds and 6 listed derivations;
- 96 attested image compounds, and the renamed pattern modifiers and heads;
- 39 example sentences;
- the counts 1462 / 472 / 990.

### 2. A respelling carries everything built on the root (2.1:G58)

`respell.ts` was the rename tool's code (ADR-0015). It now also carries:

- the attested image compounds;
- the listed derivations, both the key and the base named in the gloss line,
  with the table's order kept;
- a compound's lexicalised suffix;
- the living prose: every edition's rulings (text and examples), headword and
  image-compound notes, curated motion metaphors, and `vocab:` example tags.

Historical records keep their spellings: `history_0_8`, `legacy`, the 0.8
snapshot, the 1.0 changelog and the settled 0.8 questions. Old forms go to
`legacy.respelled_roots` and `legacy.migration_forms`, which already reserve
them for every checker. No sound-ledger rows are added, because the patch
gives no reuse policy.

### 3. New change ops

| Op                    | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `respell_as_compound` | A primal root becomes a compound of two existing headwords **in place**: its id, glosses and empty slots stay, so learner progress and overrides follow (2.1:G59). `to` is `MODIFIER+HEAD`. |
| `edit_function_word`  | A function word's `example`, for the 2.1:G46 rewrites.                                                                                                                                      |
| `spend_bank_root`     | A `reserved_root_bank` root given a meaning leaves the bank (7 in 2.1).                                                                                                                     |

`respell_root` and `retire_and_replace` now check `from` against the entry.

### 4. 2.1:G46 applied to the lexicon's own questions

The patch replaces one fronted example. The ruling was applied to every
fronted direct question the lexicon holds: 9 function-word examples and 10
example sentences, each an op that cites 2.1:G46. The question word moves to
where its answer stands, a final `ki` is added, and a time ki-word stays as a
frame. Two elliptical questions (`Ata?`, `Ata taivo?`) and the historical
`Ki-saivo lirala?` are left for the author (`review/2_1_integration.md`).

### 5. Validation

`scripts/validate_2_1.py` runs validate.py unmodified. It then re-runs the
checks the 2.0 rulings change, as `validate_2_0.py` does, with these changes:

- **Check 3** keeps the compound reading of `kezupago` (2.1:G59), and the
  2.0 kept ambiguity.
- **Check 12** finds a respelled 0.8 headword by its id.
- **Check 18** holds every respelled root to the new-root distance rule,
  against active and reserved spellings. The spellings are the author's, so
  the 35 one-edit neighbours are a WARN listed for the author, never a silent
  pass.
- **Check 25** accepts a difference from 2.0 only when it is exactly the
  recorded respelling or a recorded edit.

Result: **22 PASS, 3 WARN**.

### 6. The tools read 2.1

- The **handoff** (`liraasa_2_1_complete.json`) and the **LLM reference**
  (`liraasa_2_1_llm.json`) carry the 2.1 rulings. The dictionary flags
  `{formerly tilma}`, and the question and complement text follows G46 and
  G50.
- The **checker** implements G46 and G47 as rules, and reads NUMBER-`krono`
  (G49). An older spelling takes its 2.1 form's grammatical role.
- **Learn** diffs 2.0 → 2.1: 271 renamed, nothing added or removed. Its
  corpus is the 1.0 corpus carried into 2.1 spelling
  (`scripts/migrate_corpus_2_1.ts`, `data/corpus/*_2_1`). File names are
  kept, so text progress survives.
- The **editor data**, the **MFA export** and the **grammar guide** read 2.1.
  The guide (`Liraasa_2_1_Reference_Grammar`) teaches G46–G59 and updates its
  open questions. It reads the 2.0 song through the migration table.
- The drill's spreading pass now interleaves a word that holds most of a
  drill's remaining exercises, instead of leaving them as a run at the end.

## Consequences

- **2.1**: 1462 headwords (472 roots, 990 compounds), 10,234 forms, all
  distinct and all regenerated by rule; 106 lexicalised derivations; 391
  attested image compounds. `grammar`, `phonology` and the inflection rules
  are byte-identical to 2.0 (a test pins it); the new grammar lives in
  `rulings_2_1`.
- **Every id survives**, including those of the two roots that became
  compounds.
- **For the author**, in `review/2_1_integration.md`:
  - the one-edit neighbours (`desi` ~ `devi`, `sola` ~ `ola`, `hata` ~ `ata`…);
  - the new `kezupagolelde` split;
  - the elliptical questions;
  - two function-word examples whose `sola` now means sun;
  - whether the song gets a 2.1 version.

## Addendum: the author's review (2026-10-19)

`data/changes/2026-10-19-lexicon-2-1-review.json` applies after the 2.1
changeset:

- the `miyo` and `loya` examples are deleted, because their `sola` now means
  sun (`edit_function_word` may set `example` and `example_gloss` to null);
- `Ata taivo?` ends in `ki`;
- the new spellings stand as the patch gives them;
- the song is carried into 2.1 spelling.

The build now drops the legacy rows of in-between spellings that no edition
had (`tahamiga`).
