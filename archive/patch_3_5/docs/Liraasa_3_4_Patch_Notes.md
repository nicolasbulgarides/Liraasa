# Liraasa 3.4 — Abstraction and the human condition

*Proposed patch on top of 3.3 (2.5 + 3.0 + 3.1 + 3.3). It adds **no new roots**: every word is built from headwords that already exist.*

## What it covers

This patch targets the words you singled out, and the families they belong to:
- **States and conditions:** advantage, privilege, crisis, tradition, environment.
- **The human condition:** sex, addiction, depression, appetite, retirement.
- **Feelings and states of mind:** dislike, dread, enthusiasm, obsession, instinct.
- **Abstract verbs:** absorb, levitate, replace, improve, exchange, compare, expand.
- **Society, roles and institutions:** foreigner, specialist, pioneer, council, parliament, surgeon, scientist.
- **Culture and knowledge:** culture, economy, politics, literature, translation, electronics, canvas.

## Decisions for you

- **Sensitive words.** *amordalu* (sex) and *simvodaigo* (suicide) are neutral, literal compounds. Rename or drop them if you prefer a different register.
- **Judgment calls.** *vazrapago* (advantage, "a winning place"), *nelaramu* (culture, "a people's memory") and *pebupatu* (bottom, "foot-part") are the most metaphorical choices.
- **What's left.** Most remaining abstract gaps are bureaucratic or technical: jurisdiction, liability, implementation, certification. They belong in a later "civic and technical" pack rather than the core.
