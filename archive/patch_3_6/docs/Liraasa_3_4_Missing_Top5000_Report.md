# Liraasa 3.4: every top-5,000 word still missing, sorted into patterns

*The word pool is the union of the 5,000 most frequent content words in Brown-corpus fiction and the 5,000 most frequent general English words (wordfreq). Proper names, profanity, and inflections of words Liraasa already covers are excluded. A word counts as covered if Liraasa has it directly or through a WordNet synonym; this is the generous measure, so these are words that both measures agree are missing.*

**1,221 words are missing.** By how they can be filled:

| Bucket | Words | What it costs |
|---|---:|---|
| Already sayable: derived from a covered word | 435 | Nothing: an alias, or the existing *-san* / root-noun / *la* + adjective forms |
| Archetype on an existing head | 503 | One compound or derivation each, on a head Liraasa already has; **no roots** |
| Quality adjectives & adverbs (need a pattern or a few roots) | 115 | The only bucket that may need new roots or a ruling |
| Domain pack | 115 | Optional packs (technology, sport, politics and law, business, health, science) |
| Casual register & discourse | 36 | A small set of casual words, or recipes (*kasi* extra, *muya* multiple, *zirkisan* crazy …) |
| Grammar & numbers | 17 | Already sayable with numerals and fractions (*centa*, *una de dossa*) |

**The genuinely important gaps:**
- ***get*** is the single most important missing English word. It has no one-word equivalent, and it shouldn't have one: *rillira* (receive), *loya* (become), *venreva* (arrive) and *navdinia* (find) each cover one of its senses, and a recipe page for *get* would serve writers best.
- **Shapes.** *square*, *circle* (as a shape) and *triangle* have no word. A small shape archetype on *sovla* (shape) and *gaki* (angle) would fix them.
- ***natural*** needs a ruling (natural vs. made: *farla*), not a word.

## Reading the numbers

- **A third of the "missing" list (~36%) is already sayable.** These words derive from something Liraasa has: *naturalness* → natural, *tradition* → traditional, *practice* → practical. The fix is to make them findable (aliases, plus the G148 and G149 rules), not to coin words.
- **About 41% fill as archetype compounds on existing heads**, with no new roots:
  - **People** is the biggest family (64 words), via *-yo*, *-lirbu* and *-nela*.
  - **Events and acts** are next (56), then **time and measures** (42).
- **Only the quality adjectives (~9%) might need a few roots.** These are words like *remarkable, stubborn, cheerful, distinct, rude, steep, shy*. Many have near-equivalents among the *-san* adjectives; perhaps 20–30 of them deserve real roots.
- **Domain packs (~9%) are optional by design.** Technology, sport, government and law, business, health and science each make a natural opt-in pack.

The complete list, with category, pattern, the related existing word, the essential flag and the proposal, is in **Liraasa_3_4_Missing_Top5000_Categorized.csv**.
