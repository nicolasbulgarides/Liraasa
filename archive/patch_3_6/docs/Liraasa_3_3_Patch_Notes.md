# Liraasa 3.3 — Archetypes and the top 2,000

*This single patch combines the 3.2 archetype draft and the 3.3 completion of the top 2,000 fiction words. It applies directly on top of 3.1 and supersedes the separate 3.2 draft.*

*Proposed. Nothing is canon until you ratify it.*

## The idea

A few short **archetype roots** do most of the work. Each one combines with existing words to name whole families of things, so one new root fills many gaps.

**Two axis modifiers** turn every head noun into a pair:
- ***gudo***: the common sort, ordinary.
- ***zile***: the fine sort, fancy.

**New archetype heads** take modifiers in front of them:
- *pocu*: poultry.
- *kadu*: vehicle.
- *kaze*: gun.
- *bujo*: body joint.

**Existing heads** are reused under the same reading rule: *velma* (clothing), *nami* (food), *miru* (infused drink), *favro* (signal device), *nela* (person).

## Food, objects, places: the second wave of archetypes

**Fruit by colour** extends your own *viofuroti* (grape):
- *rubifuro* apple, *rubifuroti* cherry;
- *aurumifuro* lemon, *ojofuro* orange, *verdefuro* pear;
- *manlifuro* banana (the hand-fruit);
- *kezufuro* potato (earth-fruit).

**Plants, grains and powders:**
- *timruherba* onion (the tear-plant), *nulvaherba* garlic, *ojoherba* carrot;
- *mirumarna* rice, *aurumimarna* corn;
- *farusamni* pepper, *samnisolo* nut, *kovasamni* bean.

**Dishes:**
- *mirunami* soup, *lurvanami* sauce, *herbanami* salad, *bumrenami* sandwich;
- *zilebumre* cake (fine bread), *zilebumreti* cookie, *bumrekova* pie;
- *ertayumli* chocolate, *yumliti* candy, *furoyumli* jam, *joko* cheese;
- *lenyolavi* relish, with *lenyolavisan* "delicious, tasty".

**Things:**
- **Ring archetype:** *seldirinba* necklace, *felberinba* earring, *yugerinba* belt.
- **Objects:** *renlivelso* umbrella, *cuzabiti* pen, *conokovati* wallet, *molusemne* couch, *teruvelma* rug.
- **Rooms:** *lektazupa* living room, *kezuzupa* cellar, *lardazupa* studio.

**Places and people by derivation:**
- *namipago* restaurant, *sabepago* university, *farlapago* factory;
- *taliyo* worker, *tovoyo* owner, *dapatovoyo* customer, *lardayo* artist.

**Already in the lexicon.** Five of my proposed compounds turned out to exist already, with the same meaning: *yumlipusa* sugar, *furomiru* juice, *silikova* refrigerator, *namrevelma* hat, *yedirinba* bracelet. The archetype logic matches how you were already building words.

|---|---:|
| New roots | 9: *gudo* common · *zile* fine · *pocu* poultry · *kadu* vehicle · *kaze* gun · *bujo* joint · *tebo* early · *joko* cheese · *gubu* pig |
| New compounds | 163 |
| Listed derivations | 43 |
| Lexicalized diminutives | 21 |
| Restored forms | 2 |
| English alias index | 74 existing words made findable by their common English names |
| Recipes (phrasings that already work) | 12 |
| New function word | 1 (*eho*, "okay") |
| New rulings | 7: 3.2:G142–G147 (archetypes, meat, body units, titles, food and object archetypes, nationality) and 3.3:G148 (relational adjectives) |

After the patch: **1,836 headwords, 4,614 meaningful forms**. Tier 1 has 151 entries; tier 2 has 87, which are optional.

**Spelling budget.** All nine roots use the scarce short CVCV shape (two syllables, consonant–vowel–consonant–vowel). That is about 9 of the roughly 150 such everyday spellings left. Each one is used across many words. The 3.3 part adds **no roots at all**: every new word is built on a head that already exists.

The two measures disagree, and the truth lies between them.
- **Generous (upper bound):** this counts any WordNet synonym as a match, and adds the recipes and nationality rule. By this measure the top 2,000 words cover **99.2% of their uses in fiction**; only 38 types remain, mostly period vocabulary (redcoat, opium) or vague abstractions (activity, feature).
- **Best estimate (the table above):** this discounts synonym matches by their measured precision. It gives 87.7% of the top-2,000 word types.


## The 3.3 additions: finishing the top 2,000 with no new roots

**The leader ladder (*lirbu*).** You already had *botelirbu* (captain) and *dokralirbu* (officer); 3.3 extends the pattern:
- *dokralirbuti* sergeant / lieutenant; *dokralirbuvu* general / colonel;
- *kezulirbu* president; *lavbulirbu* bishop; *lektalirbu* politician.

**Places:**
- *bimnedomlu* building, *domluganre* yard, *salruzupa* upstairs;
- *cilnipatu* block, *kezupatu* county, *nemvapago* neighbourhood;
- *sevukova* bay, *renlilarbopago* jungle, *valminuluvu* highway, *kadulenda* railway.

**Vehicles:**
- *dapakadu* taxi, *marnakadu* tractor;
- *kadurivla* traffic, *kadusemne* parking (to park), *kaduhata* crash;
- *navukaduyo* pilot.

**Body and clothing:**
- *damvatabu* lap, *pebunazru* heel, *daigoyuge* corpse;
- *rinbavelma* skirt, *senrupavma* leather, *nulvavelma* handkerchief.

**Story and records:**
- *resotelne* comedy, *amortelne* romance, *ramucuza* record;
- *deyanole* date, *nimunole* address, *lemupatu* lesson, *bulliti* magazine.

**Manner verbs:**
- *zegovalmi* stagger, *havlisevre* choke, *dozasevre* slam;
- *vizutalva* fling, *dozademro* shove, *vizudemro* jerk;
- *tibihata* whip, *narvodalu* grope, *milnovimo* admire, *jurevilore* appreciate.

**Materials and things:**
- *zefropavma* gas, *dipopavma* wax, *larbopatu* log;
- *tibidoza* tension, *vizudesi* impulse;
- *helnati* pill, *conoti* cent, *kovavu* barrel.

**Aliases.** Thirty-nine more existing words become findable by their everyday English names. Examples: *seye* indeed, *sovelavi* satisfaction, *lideodonula* recommend, *musvasan* responsible, *lumivera* importance.

**3.3:G148, relational adjectives.** The *-san* form of any compound or derivation is available by rule, meaning "of, relating to, or marked by X": *deviserbusan* theological, *kadulendasan* railway (adjective), *resotelnesan* comic. This one ruling supplies most of the abstract "relating to" adjectives that English builds with Latinate endings, without listing them one by one.

## Decisions for you

1. **The two axis words.** *gudo* (common) and *zile* (fine) will appear in hundreds of future compounds, so be sure you like how they sound.
2. **How literal to read the axis.** *zilepocu* (duck) and *zilekadu* (carriage) are fixed, listed meanings. *zile-* + an unlisted head is readable by rule but must be flagged until it is listed (3.2:G142).
3. **Pig as a root (*gubu*).** It follows your cow, bear and frog precedent. *Farm animals* could alternatively be an archetype of their own.
4. **Still open:** period vocabulary, a handful of vague abstractions (activity, feature, instance), and exotic animals, which can be borrowed under 2.0:G45.
