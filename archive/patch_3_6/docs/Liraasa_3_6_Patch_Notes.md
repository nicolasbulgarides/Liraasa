# Liraasa 3.6 — The *2100 Dreams* patch

*Robots, the mind, the neocity. Proposed patch on top of 3.5 (all modules), built from the systematic review of the 72,000-word manuscript of* 2100 Dreams.

## What this patch does

The review found 2,065 distinct uncovered words in the novel. Of these, 236 were priority words (P1 and P2), 41 were your invented terms (P5), and 133 were names. 3.6 answers nearly all of them, mostly with compounds built from words Liraasa already has, and only **two new roots**:

| New root | Why a root and not a compound |
|---|---|
| ***kibo*** (robot; bot) | Your robots appear on almost every page, and the existing word *farvisimvodivmabiti* has seven syllables. A two-syllable everyday word was the single most important gap in the book. |
| ***garza*** (curse; damned; to swear) | The book swears often (*fuck* alone appears 43 times), and Liraasa had no coarse register at all. One harsh root now covers the interjection, the intensifier and the verb. |

Everything else is built from existing words, often with a deliberate image:
- **psychosis** *mavonibe*, "mind-fog";
- **mania** *mavofaru*, "mind-fire"; **hypomania** *mavofaruti*, "a small mind-fire";
- **bipolar** *lorevopra*, "the turning heart";
- **hologram** *lumisovla*, "light-shape";
- **firework** *farufolni*, "fire-flower";
- **dolphin** *sevureso*, "sea-laughter";
- **afterimage** *lumiramu*, "light-memory";
- **context** *senorinba*, "the ring of words around".

## New rulings

**3.6:G154 — Metric units.** Base units are compounds on patu: bondopatu (gram), lalnipatu (metre), mirupatu (litre). The diminutive -ti names the thousandth (milli-) and -vu the thousandfold (kilo-): bondopatuti milligram, bondopatuvu kilogram, lalnipatuvu kilometre. A numeral counts them: dossa bondopatuti, two milligrams. Body units (3.2:G144) remain the everyday, poetic measures.

- *penzira bondopatuti* — fifty milligrams
- *tressa lalnipatuvu* — three kilometres

**3.6:G152 — Relational adjectives from lexicalised diminutives.** 3.3:G148 also applies to lexicalised diminutives: their -san form means 'of or marked by X'. mavofaruti (hypomania) gives mavofarutisan (hypomanic); vizudemroti (tic) gives vizudemrotisan.

- *mavofaruti · mavofarutisan* — hypomania · hypomanic

**3.6:G153 — Coarse register.** Profanity is a register, not a grammar. garza is the all-purpose curse: the interjection Garza! (Fuck!, Damn!), the intensifier garzasan before a noun (fucking, damned), and the verb garzaga (to swear). rupu (waste) serves as Rupu! (Shit!). Insults are person-compounds on nela: rupunela, larnenela. Coarse words never take ascended forms, and are marked [coarse] in the lexicon.

- *Garza! Li kadu ne venreva.* — Fuck! The car isn't coming.
- *Li garzasan kibo po bakoba li tabu.* — The damned bot broke the table.

3. ***kibo*.** Is a short, friendly sound the right voice for your robots? It also gives *Sovekibo* for Zenbot.
4. **The poetic chemistry.** *lavipavma* (dopamine, "joy-substance") and *sovepavma* (serotonin, "peace-substance") are deliberately metaphorical. Keep them, or copy the scientific names instead.
