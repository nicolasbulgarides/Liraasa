**The headline: the top 2,000 need no new roots at all.** Every fillable gap sits on a head word Liraasa already has. The archetype system is doing what it was built for.

A caution on the starting point: the "covered" side of this count uses the generous synonym check. Its precision was about 71% on a hand-checked sample, so true coverage of the top 2,000 is somewhat lower than the 95.7% it implies.

**Society and rank**

The *lirbu* (leader) head you already use in *botelirbu* (captain) and *dokralirbu* (officer) gives a whole rank ladder for free:
- *dokralirbuti* sergeant; *dokralirbuvu* general;
- *kezulirbu* president (land-leader); *lavbulirbu* bishop (prayer-leader);
- *lavbusalru* steeple (prayer-height).

**Story and media**

- *resotelne* comedy (laughter-story); *amortelne* romance (love-story);
- *ramucuza* record (memory-writing);
- *deyanole* date (day-name); *nimunole* address (home-name).

**The world**

- *sevukova* bay (sea-vessel); *renlilarbopago* jungle (rain-forest);
- *kadulenda* rail (vehicle-line); *kadurivla* traffic (flow of vehicles);
- *zilekronofuro* champagne (fine wine); *dipopavma* wax (lamp-matter);
- *vizudesi* impulse (quick wanting); *tibidoza* tension (thread-strength).

## False gaps worth fixing with the alias index

About **a third of the gap (36%) is words Liraasa already has**. Their dictionary glosses just use other English words:
- **Existing words:** *seye* indeed, *sovelavi* satisfaction, *tivrota* clutch, *safasia* bathe, *lideodonula* recommend, *gudosan* casual, *bakoba* shatter.
- **Grammar:** *nayo ola* meanwhile, *futupago* someday, *vaku … follufa* frighten.

Adding these as English aliases costs nothing and closes the gap immediately.

## What's left after that

About 41 words (19% of the gap) are period vocabulary, ambiguous, or too general to lexicalize as one word:
- **Period vocabulary:** redcoat, guerrilla, opium.
- **Ambiguous:** race, mission, counter.
- **Too general:** action, activity, feature, importance.
- **Nominalized adjectives:** responsible, professional, natural, mental.

That last cluster is the one real systematic weakness left at this frequency level. These are abstract "quality" adjectives that English builds with Latinate suffixes. Liraasa may want a ruling here, for example on how *-san* adjectives make abstract nouns, rather than more words.

The full list of all 216 words, with group, action and proposal, is in **Liraasa_Top2000_Gaps_Grouped.csv**.
